About Me

I'm currently studying Electronics and Computer Science, working across coursework in circuit design and semiconductor devices, discrete mathematics and logic, and C++ programming. I like problems that sit between the two worlds — things like translating a Boolean expression into a real logic circuit, or figuring out why a rectifier isn't behaving the way the datasheet promised.

Outside of coursework, I'm curious about almost everything technical: mathematics, embedded systems, and the history of how our tools came to be. This site is itself a small project — built by hand with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and hosted on GitHub Pages.
